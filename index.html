Frequently asked questions

How do I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Use the format WIFI:T:WPA;S:YourNetworkName;P:YourPassword;; as the input text. Most phone cameras will offer to join the network when scanned.

What does error correction level mean?

Higher levels (Q, H) let the code still scan even if partly damaged or covered, at the cost of a denser pattern. Medium is fine for most uses.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to a server.
