Why use this scanner?

  • Private by design — decoding happens locally in your browser. Your image and camera feed never leave your device.

  • No signup — no account, no ads, no limits.

Frequently asked questions

Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes — drag and drop or upload any image file containing a QR code, including screenshots.

Is my camera feed uploaded anywhere?

No. The video is processed frame-by-frame locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
